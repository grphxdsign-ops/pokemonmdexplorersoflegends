## Art

Sprites and portraits are streamed at runtime from **PMDCollab SpriteCollab**
(https://sprites.pmdcollab.org, https://github.com/PMDCollab/SpriteCollab) - a
community archive of fan-made PMD-style sprites and portraits by hundreds of
artists. Per-Pokemon author credits are maintained in that repository
(each sprite/portrait folder carries its own credit metadata). Enormous thanks
to that community; this project would have placeholder blobs without them.

No official game assets (sprites, tiles, music, sound effects, text) are included
in or downloaded by this project. Tiles and UI are drawn procedurally; audio is
synthesized with WebAudio.

## Data

Species, stats, learnsets, moves, type chart, and experience tables are generated
from **PokeAPI** CSV data (https://github.com/PokeAPI/pokeapi, BSD-3-Clause).

## Design research

Mechanics documented from public community references (Bulbapedia, Serebii,
GameFAQs damage-mechanics guides, and the pmd-eos decompilation community's
damage calculator) - see docs/06_PMD_SERIES_RESEARCH.md for citations.
