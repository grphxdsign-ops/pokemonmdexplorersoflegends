Notes:

- Aura Domain has been scrapped.
- Ice Domain is renamed Frozen.
- Species spelling normalized for documentation: Orbeetle, Lilligant, and Froslass.
- Living Legends are not the original gods. They are successors in a long inheritance chain.
- The current timeline is far enough in the future that many successors have carried each Mantle over time.

- The second full domain arc after Storm is Dream.
- The partner is chosen at the start after the personality quiz.
- Team Apex eventually gives into Void corruption. After they are defeated, they begin redeeming themselves.

## First Full Domain Arc: Storm

Storm Domain is the first full domain arc after the prologue.

Detailed autonomous draft: `docs/07_STORM_DOMAIN_STORY_PASS.md`

Locked details:

- Living Legend: Luxray.
- Setting: Lightning Cliffs.
- Luxray is reckless and aggressive like lightning.
- Luxray is the first Living Legend to act on aggression as tensions begin rising between domains.
- A dark force manipulates Luxray, making him erratic and violent.
- Dusknoir's underlings, Banette and Porygon, are seen sneaking into Storm Domain and spreading something into the water.
- This creates the first major reason for the protagonist and partner to suspect Void Domain and Dusknoir.
- Team Apex does not cheat or cut corners here. They are simply known for ruthless tactics.
- The partner does not admire Team Apex in this arc and does not begin getting desperate yet.
- Arcanine saves the protagonist and partner from Luxray because Luxray is too strong for them.
- Arcanine's rescue is a trust-building ploy, because Arcanine helped create the Luxray corruption in the first place.
- Storm Domain is not a lesson arc. Its story function is to escalate suspicion toward Void Domain and Dusknoir.

## Second Full Domain Arc: Dream

Dream Domain follows Storm.

Detailed autonomous draft: `docs/08_DREAM_DOMAIN_STORY_PASS.md`

Locked details:

- Living Legend: Musharna.
- Musharna often falls asleep during dialogue.
- The sleeping habit should be characterful, funny at first, and useful to the mystery.
- Dream Domain should follow up on the nightmare-laced residue from Storm.
- Dream Domain should deepen the mystery around Nightmare, Void, and false evidence rather than fully clearing Dusknoir.

## Starter and Partner Selection

The game begins with a classic PMD-style personality quiz.

After the quiz determines or suggests the protagonist, the player chooses their partner at the start.

Design lock:

- Partner species is not a single fixed story species.
- Partner personality is fixed: ambitious, headstrong, emotional, morally grounded at first.
- Partner species should be selectable from the approved starter pool.
- Story scenes should still focus on only the protagonist and partner.

## Team Apex Redemption Path

Team Apex remains ruthless and antagonistic for much of the main story, but they are not doomed to stay villains.

Locked direction:

- Team Apex eventually gives into Void corruption at a major point in the story.
- Their corruption is partly their own fault because they seek power and status too aggressively.
- After being defeated, they begin a redemption arc.
- Their redemption should not erase harm they caused. It should begin with humility, consequences, and a willingness to help without receiving immediate praise.

## Living Legends and Guildmasters

Domain Legends are guildmaster-equivalents in this world.

Each domain has a Living Legend who acts as:

- domain leader,
- public protector,
- keeper of domain rites,
- final judge for major domain disputes,
- symbolic proof that legendhood can be inherited.

This makes every domain arc both a regional story and a test of what kind of leadership counts as legendary.

## Protagonist

The protagonist should have a mundane, quiet, and almost unremarkable life before the story begins.

They are not secretly chosen. They are not from a dramatic lineage. They are not a lost human. Their backstory should feel intentionally small.

Working backstory:

The protagonist works in a sleepy settlement supply office, doing inventory, sorting berries, copying rescue forms, mending satchels, and helping travelers restock before dungeon routes. Their life is useful but repetitive. They are known as dependable, polite, and forgettable.

Their defining traits:

- practical,
- observant,
- conflict-avoidant,
- kind in quiet ways,
- not hungry for fame,
- good at noticing what others overlook.

Their arc is not about discovering hidden destiny. It is about realizing that ordinary goodness can become legendary when tested repeatedly.

## Partner

The partner begins from a similarly ordinary background but reacts differently to it.

They hate feeling forgettable. They want their name carved into history. They are ambitious, headstrong, emotional, and restless, but they begin with solid moral grounding.

Early partner belief:

"If we become legends, then all of this finally means something."

Partner arc:

1. Starts ambitious but morally grounded.
2. Is inspired by Arcanine's heroic public image.
3. Becomes wary of Team Apex's shortcuts.
4. Feels pressure as Team Apex gains recognition faster.
5. Starts wondering whether results matter more than methods.
6. Becomes desperate near the climax.
7. Nearly makes a morally compromised choice.
8. Is pulled back by the protagonist.
9. Learns that being a legend is not about being remembered first; it is about standing for something when recognition is uncertain.

## Rival Team

Team Apex remains the rival team.

They cut corners to become legends:

- stealing credit,
- manipulating witnesses,
- abandoning weaker Pokemon,
- taking forbidden dungeon shortcuts,
- accepting suspicious aid,
- exploiting public fear of Void Domain,
- pressuring the partner by seeming to "win" faster.

Team Apex should be morally dangerous, but not flat. They represent what the partner could become if ambition outruns conscience.

## Main Villain Trio

The public suspects Dusknoir, the Void Living Legend, throughout the game.

Truth:

Dusknoir is innocent.

Dusknoir repeatedly tries to clear his own name. He also tries to drag Arcanine's name into suspicion during early interactions, but almost no one believes him because Arcanine is trusted and Dusknoir looks frightening, rules a feared domain, and is already easy for the public to blame.

The true villain trio is:

| Domain | Villain |
| --- | --- |
| Nightmare | Zoroark |
| Sun | Arcanine |
| Death | Houndoom |

Zoroark uses disguise and illusion to frame Dusknoir and the Void Domain. Arcanine uses heroic reputation and public trust to steer events. Houndoom weaponizes fear of death and endings.

Their goal:

Summon the original worshipped First Legends tied to Nightmare, Death, and Void, bringing ancient chaos back into the world and proving that the age of inherited successors should end.

Villain motivations:

- Arcanine is delusional. He saw the evil in others and now wants to restart the world anew by purging it.
- Houndoom wants the world thrown into chaos and works with Arcanine because their goals align.
- Zoroark is highly manipulative and wants to use everyone, including allies, enemies, domains, Team Apex, and public fear.

The summoned First Legends are:

- Darkrai,
- Yveltal,
- Giratina.

They are summoned against their will.

## Arcanine's Role

Arcanine is one of the first memorable Living Legends the protagonist and partner meet.

Early story role:

- Arcanine saves the protagonist and partner from a dangerous dungeon.
- The rescue should be spectacular, brave, and emotionally sincere on the surface.
- This makes Arcanine a model of heroism for both leads, especially the partner.

Late story truth:

Arcanine is part of the villain trio. His public heroism is real enough to be believable, but it is also curated. He is delusional rather than merely selfish: after seeing evil in others, he concluded that the current world is too compromised to save and must be purged so it can begin again.

## Void Domain Framing

Void Domain members often appear to cause havoc around the world.

This should create a long suspicion arc:

- Void-aligned Pokemon appear near disasters.
- Witnesses report Dusknoir or Void agents.
- Domains blame Void for growing unrest.
- Team Apex uses the suspicion to gain easy popularity.
- Dusknoir becomes isolated, defensive, and hard to trust.
- Dusknoir tries to defend himself and point suspicion toward Arcanine, but this backfires because the world trusts Arcanine's heroic image and fears Dusknoir's appearance and domain.

Twist:

Many of these incidents are Zoroark's disguises or illusions. Dusknoir never committed the central crimes.

## Climax

Location: Tower of Legends

The Tower of Legends is the tallest dungeon in the world. At its summit, a First Legend can be summoned.

The protagonist and partner believe they are climbing the tower to stop Dusknoir from summoning Giratina.

At the top:

1. "Dusknoir" drops the illusion and is revealed as Zoroark.
2. Arcanine and Houndoom reveal themselves.
3. The protagonist and partner battle the three villains.
4. After the villains are defeated, the summoning has already gone too far.
5. Yveltal, Darkrai, and Giratina are summoned against their will.
6. Allies made throughout the game arrive.
7. The allies help contain and unsummon the First Legends in a cutscene-scale sequence rather than a normal player-controlled battle.
8. Before being unsummoned, Yveltal, Darkrai, and Giratina launch one last attack at the partner.
9. The protagonist jumps in front and takes the full attack.
10. The First Legends are defeated and unsummoned.
11. The protagonist lies still.

## Resurrection Scene

Everyone mourns the protagonist.

Flashbacks appear, showing the protagonist's quiet good actions across the game:

- choosing mercy,
- helping ordinary Pokemon,
- grounding the partner,
- resolving domain conflicts,
- earning trust without chasing praise,
- proving that small acts became a legend over time.

Then a new summoning begins. Everyone prepares for another fight.

Instead, Xerneas, Cresselia, and Arceus appear.

They praise the protagonist:

- Xerneas grants life.
- Cresselia grants spirit.
- Arceus grants power.

The three vanish. The protagonist rises. Everyone celebrates and returns home.

This is the end of the main game. Postgame starts from here.
