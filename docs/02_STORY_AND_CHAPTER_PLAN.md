# Story and Chapter Plan

## Story Premise

The current world lives long after the age of the First Legends. The original legendary and mythical Pokemon were ancient domain gods, but their Mantles have passed down through successors for generations.

Living Legends now lead the domains as guildmaster-equivalents. They are respected, feared, or worshipped depending on the domain.

The protagonist and partner start as ordinary Pokemon with ordinary lives. They are inspired by Arcanine, the Sun Living Legend, after he saves them from a dungeon. They begin a journey to earn Renown and become legends.

As they travel, the domains fall into distrust. Void Domain is blamed for disasters, Team Apex gains fame through shortcuts, and the partner slowly becomes tempted by the idea that being remembered may matter more than doing things properly.

The truth is that Zoroark, Arcanine, and Houndoom are causing the chaos. Zoroark frames Dusknoir and the Void Domain through illusions. The trio intends to summon Darkrai, Yveltal, and Giratina at the Tower of Legends.

## Protagonist Backstory

Working version:

The protagonist grew up in a quiet settlement where nothing important seemed to happen. Their daily work involved sorting supplies, labeling berries, copying rescue requests, mending satchels, sweeping the depot, and helping travelers prepare for safe routes.

They are not unhappy, but their life has no spark of grandeur. Others see them as reliable but ordinary. The protagonist expects to remain ordinary.

Their strength is mundane goodness:

- they remember small details,
- they notice when someone is missing,
- they help before being asked,
- they do unglamorous work well,
- they stay calm when the partner gets reckless.

This gives the final resurrection scene weight. The flashbacks should show that the protagonist's legend was built from small choices, not destiny.

## Partner Backstory

Working version:

The partner comes from a similarly quiet life. They have done errands, low-level guild chores, and local help work. Unlike the protagonist, they cannot stand the idea of staying forgettable.

They collect stories about Living Legends and First Legends. They memorize heroic rescue tales. They visit old name-stones and imagine their own name carved there.

They convince the protagonist to join them because the protagonist is the one Pokemon they trust to keep them grounded.

Early partner pitch:

"Come with me. Not because we're special yet. Because if we stay here, we never will be."

## Arcanine Rescue Scene

Arcanine is introduced as the Sun Living Legend and one of the most inspiring figures in the world.

Scene purpose:

- Establish Living Legends as real, impressive, and emotionally powerful.
- Give the partner a heroic ideal.
- Make the later betrayal hurt.

Event outline:

1. Protagonist and partner enter a beginner dungeon for a small rescue or delivery.
2. The dungeon destabilizes beyond their ability.
3. Fire, light, or sun-themed danger blocks escape.
4. Arcanine arrives with overwhelming confidence and protects them.
5. Arcanine saves trapped civilians or wild Pokemon too, proving the rescue is not only about the heroes.
6. Arcanine tells them that legends are made by answering when the world calls.
7. The partner is visibly changed by the encounter.

Foreshadowing:

- Arcanine is too polished.
- Witnesses praise him almost automatically.
- He asks pointed questions about ambition.
- He subtly praises being remembered more than serving quietly.

## Main Act Outline

### Act 1: The Small Life

- Show the protagonist's mundane routine.
- Introduce the partner's ambition.
- Establish the local settlement and small rescue work.
- Send both into the first dungeon.
- Arcanine saves them.
- They choose to leave home and pursue legendhood.

### Act 2: First Renown

- The pair completes small missions.
- They learn about domain Mantles and Living Legends.
- They hear rumors of Void Domain trouble.
- Team Apex appears and earns attention through aggressive methods.
- The partner initially rejects Team Apex's behavior.

### Act 3: Domain Pilgrimage

- The protagonists travel between domains.
- Each domain has a main conflict, a Living Legend, and optional quests.
- The domains begin blaming one another for disasters.
- Void Domain becomes a common scapegoat.
- Zoroark's illusions create false evidence.
- Dusknoir appears suspicious but consistently avoids open cruelty.

### Act 4: Apex Pressure

- Team Apex gains fame faster.
- The partner becomes frustrated by slow, ethical progress.
- Optional choices can either steady or worsen the partner's moral drift.
- Arcanine continues to act as a mentor figure.
- Houndoom and Zoroark appear indirectly through domain crises.

### Act 5: The Void Accusation

- Evidence appears to prove Dusknoir is behind the chaos.
- Domains prepare for open conflict.
- The protagonist and partner climb toward the Tower of Legends to stop Dusknoir.
- Team Apex may interfere, assist, or crumble depending on prior choices.

### Act 6: Tower of Legends

- The player climbs the tallest dungeon.
- The partner is tempted to take a shortcut or make a morally compromised choice.
- The protagonist brings them back.
- At the summit, "Dusknoir" is revealed as Zoroark.
- Arcanine and Houndoom reveal themselves.
- The protagonist and partner battle the villain trio.

### Act 7: The Forced Summoning

- The summoning completes despite the trio's defeat.
- Darkrai, Yveltal, and Giratina are summoned against their will.
- Allies from previous domain arcs arrive.
- The allies help contain the First Legends in a cutscene sequence.
- The First Legends launch one last attack at the partner.
- The protagonist takes the hit.
- The First Legends are unsummoned.
- The protagonist appears dead.

### Act 8: Blessing of Legends

- Everyone mourns.
- Flashbacks replay the protagonist's quiet good actions.
- Xerneas, Cresselia, and Arceus are summoned.
- Xerneas grants life.
- Cresselia grants spirit.
- Arceus grants power.
- The protagonist rises.
- The main game ends with the team returning home.
- Postgame begins.

## Postgame Starting Point

Postgame starts after the protagonist's resurrection.

Possible postgame focuses:

- repair relations between domains,
- clear the names of Dusknoir and Void Domain,
- decide consequences for Arcanine, Houndoom, and Zoroark,
- recruit or challenge legendary Pokemon,
- explore deeper Tower of Legends floors,
- pursue grandmaster skill ranks,
- unlock alternate Mantle trials,
- reveal why Arceus answered the final summon.
