# Storm Domain Story Pass

Status: autonomous draft based on locked Round 2 canon.

## Arc Title

Storm Over Lightning Cliffs

## Arc Purpose

Storm Domain is the first full domain arc after the prologue. It should not teach a tidy moral lesson. Its purpose is to make the protagonist and partner believe Void Domain and Dusknoir may truly be dangerous.

This arc should accomplish five things:

1. Show that Living Legends can become frighteningly powerful when unstable.
2. Establish Luxray as reckless and aggressive like lightning.
3. Make Banette and Porygon look guilty in a public way.
4. Let Team Apex appear ruthless without making the partner admire them yet.
5. Give Arcanine another heroic public rescue that quietly deepens his manipulation.

## Setting

Storm Domain is built around the Lightning Cliffs: black stone ridges, high bridges, iron-bell towers, rain-slick paths, charged springs, and cliffside settlements that use lightning rods to power signal beacons.

Primary town:

Thunderhead Crossing

This town sits between cliff paths and storm channels. Its people respect decisive action because hesitation can get someone killed during a lightning surge.

Important places:

- Thunderhead Crossing: cliffside town and mission hub.
- Static Run: early dungeon path where small electric disturbances begin.
- Raincoil Aqueduct: water system carrying charged springwater through the cliffs.
- Stormcall Perch: high shrine where Luxray receives reports and issues domain orders.
- Lightning Crown Summit: final Storm arc dungeon area where Luxray becomes fully violent.

## Luxray

Luxray is the Storm Living Legend.

Personality:

- reckless,
- aggressive,
- direct,
- proud,
- protective in a harsh way,
- quick to believe action is better than debate.

Luxray is not evil. He is built to act first, and the dark force pushes that trait into dangerous violence.

Public reputation:

Storm Domain citizens trust Luxray because storms punish hesitation. They may fear his temper, but they respect his speed and certainty.

Corrupted behavior:

- orders patrols into neighboring routes,
- accuses other domains before proof is complete,
- treats hesitation as disloyalty,
- uses intimidation to force confessions,
- nearly attacks the protagonist and partner when they question the evidence.

## Banette and Porygon

Autonomous story decision:

Banette and Porygon are real Void Domain agents, but they are not poisoning the water.

They were sent by Dusknoir to investigate strange shadow residue appearing near the Lightning Cliffs. They sneak into the Raincoil Aqueduct because Dusknoir knows no one will cooperate with Void openly.

What they are actually doing:

- Porygon is scanning the aqueduct for unnatural distortion patterns.
- Banette is pouring a stabilizing sealant into the water to slow the corruption.

Why they look guilty:

- The sealant is dark and smoke-like.
- They flee when discovered because they know Void agents will not get a fair hearing.
- Zoroark plants a matching empty vial in a public square.
- Witnesses only see "Dusknoir's underlings spreading something into the water."

This gives the story a strong false clue without making Dusknoir's side incompetent or secretly evil.

## The Dark Force

Autonomous story decision:

Luxray is affected by nightmare-laced distortion residue.

This lets Zoroark and Houndoom both have fingerprints on the incident:

- Zoroark shapes the hallucinations and false appearances.
- Houndoom supplies death-domain pressure that makes the corruption feel urgent and hostile.
- Arcanine chooses the target and timing so he can later arrive as the savior.

Symptoms:

- Luxray hears thunder as voices.
- Reflections in charged water show Void symbols.
- Luxray sees harmless hesitation as conspiracy.
- Lightning around him turns black-blue during outbursts.

## Team Apex Role

Team Apex does not cheat in this arc.

They act as brutal problem-solvers:

- subdue panicking Pokemon too roughly,
- force open blocked routes,
- publicly criticize the protagonist's caution,
- win praise for speed even when their methods hurt trust.

The partner does not admire them yet. Instead, the partner is angry that Team Apex gets applause for being ruthless while the protagonist's team is still trying to understand what is happening.

This plants pressure without starting the partner's desperation arc too early.

## Arcanine's Role

Arcanine arrives near the climax and saves the protagonist and partner from Luxray.

Public version:

Arcanine hears Storm Domain is in danger, rushes to the Lightning Cliffs, and bravely stands between young adventurers and an out-of-control Living Legend.

Secret truth:

Arcanine helped choose Luxray as the first destabilized Living Legend because Storm Domain would react loudly and violently. He needed an incident public enough to make all domains afraid.

How he saves them:

Arcanine does not defeat Luxray outright. He creates a blazing ring of sunlight and heat that breaks Luxray's line of attack long enough for others to pull the protagonist and partner away.

This keeps Luxray powerful and prevents Arcanine from seeming too obviously staged.

Foreshadowing:

- Arcanine knows too quickly where to stand.
- He refers to Luxray's condition as "corruption" before anyone has explained it.
- He gently discourages the protagonist from asking why Banette and Porygon were in the aqueduct.
- He praises the partner's courage in a way that subtly connects bravery with being remembered.

## Chapter Flow

### Chapter 1: Rumor on the Ridge

The protagonist and partner arrive at Thunderhead Crossing after hearing Storm Domain patrols are blocking rescue routes.

Initial beats:

- NPCs argue about Void sightings.
- Team Apex returns from a route with injured outlaws tied up too harshly.
- Luxray gives an aggressive public speech promising action.
- The partner dislikes Team Apex's methods but notices the crowd respects results.

### Chapter 2: Static Run

The first Storm dungeon introduces charged water, lightning traps, and frightened Pokemon.

Objective:

Find missing ridge workers.

Clue:

The workers report seeing "a doll-shadow and a floating machine" near the aqueduct.

### Chapter 3: Raincoil Aqueduct

The protagonist and partner investigate the water system.

They glimpse Banette and Porygon pouring dark sealant into the water. Before they can explain, Team Apex crashes in loudly, forcing the Void agents to flee.

Result:

Storm Domain now has public witnesses and a physical vial that seems to implicate Void.

### Chapter 4: Luxray's Judgment

Luxray declares that Void Domain has attacked Storm Domain.

The protagonist and partner hesitate because the evidence feels too clean. Luxray interprets that hesitation as weakness or possible sympathy for Void.

Team Apex supports aggressive action, but still without cheating.

### Chapter 5: Lightning Crown Summit

Luxray becomes fully unstable and tries to hunt down anyone connected to the investigation.

The protagonist and partner reach the summit to stop further violence, but Luxray overwhelms them.

Arcanine arrives and saves them.

Ending beat:

The crowd cheers Arcanine. The protagonist sees Banette watching silently from a distant ridge before disappearing into the rain.

## End State

By the end of the Storm arc:

- The protagonist and partner suspect Dusknoir.
- Luxray is alive but unstable, ashamed, or removed from public action temporarily.
- Arcanine becomes even more trusted.
- Team Apex gains reputation as a ruthless but effective team.
- Banette and Porygon remain unresolved false clues.
- The partner is frustrated, but not yet tempted by Team Apex.
- Domain tensions become real political danger.
